For decades, if a Bollywood heroine broke into song on screen, her voice almost always shared one distinct quality: a light, soaring, crystalline high pitch.

But voice isn't a rigid box. Every singer brings a unique mix of pitch, texture, resonance, and song emotion. So how did we get from one dominant sound to the rich, varied vocal palette we hear today?

SOPRANO: High, light, soaring, crystalline tone
MEZZO-SOPRANO: Warm, versatile, rich middle-register
CONTRALTO: Deep, earthy, grounded, low-frequency texture
